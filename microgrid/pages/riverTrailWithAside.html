Lawrence River Trail

Meandering along the Kaw River, the River Trail is a wooded ten-mile single lane loop located in North Lawrence. It's rated easy, but there are plenty of tight turns, rocks, logs, and obstacles to keep riders alert and enjoying the ride.

Directions

On the north side of the Mass Street Bridge, go east on Elm Street. Turn right on 8th Street and look for the trailhead parking area. From there you can start your adventure along the scenic river trail.

Trail Rules

  1. Only ride when the trail is open.
  2. Riders go clockwise; hikers go the opposite direction.
  3. Hikers yield to riders.
  4. Stay on marked trails.
  5. Respect wildlife and nature.
