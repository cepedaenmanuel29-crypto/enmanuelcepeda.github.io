Food Pyramid Guide
Food Group Recommended Servings Examples
Grains 6-11 servings Bread, Rice, Pasta
Vegetables 3-5 servings Carrots, Broccoli, Spinach
Fruits 2-4 servings Apples, Bananas, Oranges
Dairy 2-3 servings Milk, Cheese, Yogurt
Protein 2-3 servings Chicken, Fish, Beans
Fats & Sweets Use Sparingly Candy, Soda, Butter
