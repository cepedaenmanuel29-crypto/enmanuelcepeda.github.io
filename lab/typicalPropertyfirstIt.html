Summertime Power in a Small (3000 sqft) Shop or Restaurant

Property Description

This case study analyzes the electrical system of a small two-story downtown property in Lawrence. The building occupies one lot measuring 25 feet wide by 120 feet deep, totaling approximately 3000 square feet.

Typical System Description

  • Peak electrical consumption at 4:00 PM: 7.5 kW
  • Peak HVAC consumption: 4 kW
  • At noon: 2 kW for cooling spaces + 2 kW for chilled water storage
  • Chilled water stored from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM
  • Provides 3 hours of maximum evening cooling
  • Solar collectors rated at 18 kW
  • Solar capacity factor: 15%
  • Annual savings from solar: $3,300

Assumptions

  • Electricity cost assumed: $0.14 per kWh
  • HVAC unit: 5-ton Carrier 50YER ground-source heat pump
  • Chilled water tank: 2000 gallon Westheffer tank
  • Battery storage: 18 kWh M100 Aquion sodium-ion battery

Conclusion

This system improves efficiency by using solar energy, thermal storage, and battery storage to reduce peak demand and operating costs. The design minimizes rooftop clutter while maximizing energy efficiency.
